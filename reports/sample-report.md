## Executive Summary

- Artificial intelligence is changing education through personalized learning, automated feedback, and intelligent tutoring systems.
- Responsible use of AI in education requires privacy protection, transparent systems, and human oversight.
- Future classrooms may use AI to support lesson planning, formative assessment, accessibility, and multilingual learning.

## Key Findings

- AI tools can help teachers reduce repetitive work and support students with adaptive practice.
- These systems can identify learning gaps and recommend content based on each student's progress.
- Educators need training to understand both the benefits and limitations of AI systems.
